## 4. Admission Procedure (group-wide, applies to Hyderabad too)

1. **Collect form in person:** Pick up the form & prospectus at the respective IIHM campus for a fee of ₹600 (cash / cheque / demand draft in favour of "International Institute of Hotel Management," payable at the local campus city). Submit filled form in person or by post.
2. **Download and post:** Download the form from the site, fill it, and submit (in person or by post) with the ₹600 fee as above.
3. **Apply online (eCHAT):** Register on https://echat.elink.in, fill the form, and pay online. Note: if paid online, the physical prospectus is mailed to the applicant.
4. **eCHAT** is IIHM's admission interview/counselling process — the site publishes upcoming eCHAT dates and lets applicants check eCHAT results online.
5. Admissions for the 2026 session were open at time of research.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (group-wide, source: iihm.ac.in FAQs)

- **Course duration:** 3-year Bachelor's degree in Hospitality Management; also a 12-month diploma and 9/6/3-month certificate courses.
- **Equivalence:** IIHM's hospitality management course is treated as equivalent to a graduation degree.
- **Stream requirement:** Students from any stream — Science, Arts, or Commerce — are eligible.
- **Age criteria:** Roughly 18–22.
- **Minimum qualification:** Class 12 marksheet from a recognised board.
- **After graduation:** Hotel management study is possible after completing a prior graduation degree too.
- **Education loans:** Banks do provide education loans for hotel management studies.
